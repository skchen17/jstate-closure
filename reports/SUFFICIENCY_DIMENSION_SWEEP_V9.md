## Conditional-comparator amendment

For v9, `J + C_d + residual(raw | C_d)` is evaluated by the frozen full-information ceiling, which is informationally equivalent to compact coordinates plus their orthogonal complement and avoids entering the compact coordinates twice in a ridge model. The v8 value near `0.063` used duplicated compact and full coordinates; it is therefore not directly comparable to the v9 512/576/599D values. The v9 results establish a near-zero conditional gain within the tested 512D+ range, but do not localize a transition below 512D under the corrected comparator.

The conditional-gain figure intentionally excludes the v8 points below 512D because they used the older duplicated-coordinate comparator.
